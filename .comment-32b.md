## The generalisation is ruled: recorded in ADR 0006, and one more gap filed

Owner's words, 2026-11-12: *"ok with the engine generalization."* [ADR 0006](../blob/main/docs/adr/0006-machine-loop-subscriptions.md)
now carries a dated revision: invariant 3 becomes "a suspended coroutine owns at least one pending wake …
fire-once, the first to fire owns the resume, the rest are stale", invariant 4 gains the sequence
discriminator, and the two invariants are amended in place so the ADR does not contradict itself.

Assertion 2 was restated with it and **strengthened again after the fact**: the cheap side runs every
batch (the chip's own armed counter against the engine's live-wake count), and a full recount rebuilt
from the queue and the subscription store runs every 256 batches, so the two sides stay independent
without walking a 15 000-entry queue per instant. `scripts/validate.sh` stays green on it (Debug, and
the boot's first frames).

### Third gap, found while auditing what G5 actually counts

`design/crossings.md` §2 gives `DE` **200 records a frame and 400 firings** (two subscribers). The build
cannot reproduce that shape, and the reason is structural rather than a bug:

- a level is only expressible in an append-only log as **two** records — assert and release — so a model
  that answers "what is `DE` at instant *t*" from the log emits **400 records a frame**, not 200;
- with one record per line the release would have to be implied by the next line's assert, which is not
  a log query any more.

The build therefore reports 200 asserts + 200 releases and gates on the **assert count**, and Video
reads the level from the log instead of subscribing (it also cannot subscribe to `DE` while it is
waiting on a group delivery — or, with the revision above, it could, but the level read is what the
design says `BLANK` gets and it is one wake fewer).

So G5's `DE` row needs one of three rulings before it can be green as written:

1. **Count the edges:** the row becomes "200 `DE` assert records a frame, 400 firings" and the release is
   a record too (what the code does today).
2. **One record per line carrying the window:** `DE` becomes one record a line whose lanes carry the
   window's two instants, and the level query resolves the window arithmetically (fewer records, a
   composite value).
3. **Keep 200 records and let Video and Timer B subscribe** to that single record (the release is then
   invisible, which is fine for both subscribers).

Recommendation: **(1)** — it is what an append-only log can honestly say, it makes the release a first
class fact, and it costs one line in the catalogue rather than a new record shape.
