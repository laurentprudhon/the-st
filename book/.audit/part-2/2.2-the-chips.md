## What this audit changed

- Paragraph 1: "the only one that carries the production part numbers" became "of the two documents that describe it,
  only this one" — the 1989 BOM's silence is the reason, and that is now said.
- Paragraph 6: the fetch path was described as the Shifter addressing DRAM. It does not: it requests a slot and is
  loaded. Rewritten to `SREQ`/`SLOAD`/`MD`, which is what §4.2 draws.
- Paragraph 10: one sentence was left duplicated by the rewrite above and removed.

## What this audit did not settle, and where it is recorded

The chapter states four absences (blitter population, Super Hi-Res, RAM configuration, the 1989 BOM) as
documented facts. It does not say which *revision* of `CA4003290` dropped the blitter, because the inventory does not
say it either — it says only that later boards fold the function into the GSTMCU (§6.8). That is a board-generation
question and belongs to 2.1; the chapter hands it over explicitly in §6 rather than guessing a revision number.
