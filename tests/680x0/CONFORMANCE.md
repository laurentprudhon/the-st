# The conformance set: what has been checked against hardware, and where the corpora disagree

The 68000 core is measured against emulator-generated corpora (`SingleStepTests/680x0` from Tom Harte's CLK,
`SingleStepTests/m68000` from MAME's microcoded core). Neither is hardware. This document records the
sources that **are**, what each one actually checks, and where the two corpora disagree with them — so that
"the corpus says" is never mistaken for "the chip does".

## 1. Tiers

| Tier | What it means | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | captured from silicon | **flamewing's BCD verifier** — exhaustive ABCD/SBCD/NBCD tables whose ROM passes on a Model 1 and a Model 3 VA2 Sega Genesis |
| **T2** | emulator corrected *on* silicon | **WinUAE's `cputest` core** — Toni Wilen corrects it by running each test on a real 7 MHz Amiga until the hardware agrees (*"All 68000 tests are 100% confirmed, including full cycle-count support"*, 2020) |
| **T3** | emulator only | MAME's microcoded core, CLK, Musashi — and therefore **both corpora we gate on** |

Tiers follow `alewman/m68000-python` (`docs/validation.md`, "the tier rule"), pinned `c827cca`, which built and
ran both T2/T3 referees rather than reading their sources. A T3 source can raise a question; it cannot settle
one.

## 2. What is vendored here

### `conformance/flamewing-bcd/` — T1

`bcd-table.bin`, 1 050 624 bytes — the exact table the verifier's ROM embeds and real hardware validated.
Generated by `bcd-gen` in `flamewing/68k-bcd-verifier` @ `39a01be` (GPL-3.0; the table is data, not code,
and is derived from that generator's model whose ROM passed on hardware). Layout, 2 bytes per entry
`[flags, result]`, `flags = X<<4 | N<<3 | Z<<2 | V<<1 | C`:

| instruction | entries | offset | index |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ABCD Dy,Dx` | 262 144 | 0 | `((src*256 + dst)*4 + X*2 + Z) * 2` |
| `SBCD Dy,Dx` | 262 144 | 524 288 | same, + 524 288 |
| `NBCD Dx` | 1 024 | 1 048 576 | `((dst*4 + X*2 + Z) * 2) + 1 048 576` |

Self-check (any reader must reproduce these): `ABCD $09+$01, X=0,Z=0` → `$10`, flags `00000`;
`SBCD $00−$01, X=0,Z=0` → `$99`, flags `11001` (X,N,C).

### `conformance/winuae-cputest/` — T2

`cputestgen.ini` and `readme.txt`, from `tonioni/WinUAE` @ `1977af5` (GPL-2.0+). The `.ini` is the preset list
that says *which cases the tester generates* — every instruction and addressing mode, trace on in a second
round, AESRC/AEDST (odd operands), ODDSTK, ODDEXC (odd vectors), IRQ/ODDIRQ, BEPR/BEBR/BESRC/BEDST/BESRCW/BEDSTW
(bus errors). The expected values are **not** vendored: they come from the tester core that `gencpu` emits when
built with `CPU_TESTER`, and the comparison happens on the Amiga side against real hardware. The build recipe
is in the readme and was followed on Linux by the project cited above; wiring it up here is ticket **#72**.

## 3. The ledger — measured here, 2026-11-18

### T1 against both corpora (exhaustive: every case the corpus has)

Operands were read out of each record's own data-space transactions (function code 5), the byte taken from the
lane the corpus drives (`CLK` puts a byte in the low lane; `m68000` follows the chip — `$AB00` for a byte at an
even address). Records whose operands never reached the bus (address error, no write) are counted, not judged.

| corpus | ABCD | SBCD | NBCD | judged | address-error records |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `680x0` (CLK) | 8 065 / 8 065 | 8 065 / 8 065 | 8 065 / 8 065 | **24 195 / 24 195** | 0 |
| `m68000` (MAME) | 1 858 / 1 858 | 1 854 / 1 854 | 1 423 / 1 423 | **5 135 / 5 135** | 2 365 |

**Zero disagreements.** On the only instruction family where silicon-captured expectations exist, both corpora
are exactly right — so `FINDINGS.md`'s BCD rows are hardware-backed, not merely corpus-backed, and the
"undefined N and V" question (PRM: `U`) is settled by T1, not by either corpus.

### T2 against both corpora (cited, not re-run here)

From `alewman/m68000-python` @ `c827cca`, `docs/referees.md`, a run of 2026-09-21 of WinUAE's tester core
(`1977af5`) over **both** corpora in full:

- against `m68000`: **308 416 of 314 988** judged cases agree (97.9 %); 89 complete files; every one of the
  6 572 disagreements is an address error or a clock difference of exactly 2 — *"no ordinary case disagrees"*.
- against `680x0`: agrees on **all 779 253** cases it can judge where CLK and MAME's core agree.

The disagreement families, gate (MAME) against WinUAE:

| cases | differs | MAME | WinUAE |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 4 330 | `An` after a word `(An)+` operand address error (32 files) | moved by 2 | not moved |
| 115 | `An` after `CMPM.L (An)+,(An)+` faults | 2 further | 2 less |
| 762 | stacked PC of `JSR (d16,An)/(d8,An,Xn)/(d16,PC)/(d8,PC,Xn)` to an odd target | instruction + 4 | instruction + 2 |
| 632 | stacked PC of `DBcc` to an odd target | instruction + 4 | target + 2 |
| 306 | stacked PC of `MOVEM (d8,An,Xn)/(d8,PC,Xn)` at an odd address | 4 more | 4 less |
| 228 | clocks of `MOVE to -(An)` whose write faults | n | n + 2 |
| 199 | clocks of `CHK` trapping with `Dn < 0` when `bound − Dn` overflows 16 bits | 8 internal | 10 internal |

**And a fourth, measured here:** for `MOVEM.l #, (An)` (two words) faulting on its store, CLK stacks
`opcode + 2` and MAME stacks `opcode + 6` (its `m_au`). Four bytes apart, in the family ticket #70 fixes.

## 4. What this means

- **BCD is closed**: T1 confirms both corpora; nothing to arbitrate.
- **The address-error frame's stacked PC is contested** in at least three families, and the T2 evidence does not
  side with one corpus throughout. Any core change there must say which source decided it — a value copied
  from whichever corpus was run last is a guess with a checksum.
- Neither corpus models bus error (BERR) at all; T2 does, and disagrees with both. That is the next frontier
  after the frames, not part of them.
